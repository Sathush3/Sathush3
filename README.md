## About Me

I am a Software Engineer II with over five years of experience across mobile engineering and applied artificial intelligence.

I build and maintain production iOS and Android applications using React Native, Flutter, TypeScript, Dart, and native iOS technologies. My professional work includes connected IoT devices, BLE and Wi-Fi communication, sensor analytics, offline data handling, complex visualizations, media processing, performance optimization, and production releases.

I am currently completing a Master of Artificial Intelligence at Monash University Malaysia. My studies include artificial intelligence, intelligent agents, multi-agent systems, search and planning, machine learning, deep learning, data science, forecasting, and model evaluation.

I also experiment with AI-supported development workflows, agent orchestration, automation systems, local models, and practical applications of AI in software engineering.
